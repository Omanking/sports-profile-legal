Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Before publishing: Replace omanking@powaallc.com with a working support email on both pages. Review all policy statements against the actual production app and services; remove or revise anything inaccurate.

This Privacy Policy explains how Sports Profile ("Sports Profile," "we," "our") handles information when people use the Sports Profile mobile application and related services. For privacy questions, contact omanking@powaallc.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: information supplied to create and manage an account, such as email address, name, username, account role and, where provided, phone or other contact details.
  • Profile and sports information: player, coach, referee and team profiles; positions, affiliations, performance statistics, game requests, lineups, reviews and related information users submit.
  • Photos, videos and other content: profile pictures, gallery media, messages, comments, and other content uploaded or sent through the app.
  • Location: city or region information supplied by users and, with device permission, location information used to support nearby discovery. The precision and retention of device location depend on the implemented feature and permissions.
  • Technical information: authentication/session information and operational logs or diagnostics necessary to run, secure and troubleshoot the service, where collected.

How we use information

We use information to create accounts, authenticate users, display profiles and sports activity, provide discovery and messaging, coordinate teams and games, process stats approval workflows, respond to support requests, protect the service from abuse, and maintain app reliability.

Visibility and sharing

Information users choose to publish in profiles, team pages, reviews, games or discovery may be visible to other app users. Messages and private workflows are intended for their relevant participants, subject to app functionality and access controls. We use service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database and storage. We may disclose information when legally required or to address safety and abuse concerns.

Permissions and choices

Location access is optional and can be changed through device settings. Users can manage the information they submit and may request access, correction or deletion by contacting omanking@powaallc.com. Where available, in-app account settings may also provide these controls.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of an account and associated personal data, contact omanking@powaallc.com with the subject “Sports Profile Account Deletion.” We may verify account ownership before processing a request. Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, dispute resolution or legitimate recordkeeping. Confirm that this deletion process is actually implemented before publishing.

Data retention and security

We retain information while needed to operate the service and for legitimate security or legal purposes, and seek to protect it through access controls and encrypted network communications. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Sports Profile should be used only in accordance with the app's published age requirements and applicable law. If you believe a child's information has been collected inappropriately, contact us to request review and removal.

International processing

Information may be processed in locations where our infrastructure and service providers operate, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here and update its effective date.

Contact

Sports Profile privacy inquiries: omanking@powaallc.com.
