SPORTS PROFILE LEGAL PAGES

1. IMPORTANT: Replace SUPPORT_EMAIL_REPLACE_ME in privacy-policy.html and support.html with your real working email.
2. Read and adjust the Privacy Policy so every statement accurately matches your app, data retention, permissions and deletion process. This is a starting draft, not legal advice.
3. Upload index.html, privacy-policy.html and support.html to the ROOT of the PUBLIC GitHub repository sports-profile-legal. Do not upload README.txt unless desired.
4. GitHub repository > Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
5. After publication, open the URLs in a private browser to verify:
https://omanking.github.io/sports-profile-legal/privacy-policy.html
https://omanking.github.io/sports-profile-legal/support.html
6. Enter the verified URLs in App Store Connect.
7. The app must have an actual account deletion workflow that meets Apple requirements; a support email alone may not satisfy in-app deletion requirements.
